# Ethiopia‑first E‑commerce: Nationwide + Geo‑Nearby + All Wallets

### Goals

- Enable any merchant nationwide to sell; recommend nearby merchants for faster, cheaper delivery.
- Support unit‑aware commerce (kg/L/m/piece, variable weight) with transparent per‑unit pricing.
- Payments at launch: Telebirr, Chapa, Amole/CBE Birr, plus COD; escrow by default.
- Nationwide delivery via regional partners; OTP/photo proof; SMS fallbacks for low data.

### Scope aligned to your choices

- Delivery: Nationwide merchant onboarding and sales; logistics through multiple regional carriers and pickup points; recommendations prioritize proximity, stock, rating, and lead time.
- Payments: Integrate Telebirr (escrow), Chapa (cards/banks/wallets), Amole/CBE Birr, COD. Sandbox first; no customer card needed for launch flows.

### Architecture

- Modular monolith (single deploy) with clear modules: Identity & KYC, Merchant, Catalog, Inventory, Orders, Payments & Escrow, Logistics, Loyalty & Reviews, Messaging.
- PostgreSQL (single DB) + Redis (queues/cache); object storage for media; CDN for images.
- Web PWA (SSR/SPA hybrid) with Amharic default, Oromo/Tigrinya next; SMS/USSD fallbacks for critical steps.

### Data model highlights

- Merchant (profile, KYC tier, geo: lat/lon, service areas)
- Product → SKU (unitType, unitIncrement, packageSize, pricePerCanonicalUnit)
- InventoryLot (supports variable weight/packaging, expiry/lot metadata)
- Order, OrderItem (quantity + unitType, variable‑weight settlement)
- Shipment (carrier, OTP, events, photo proof)
- Payment (provider, escrowState), Payout
- Review (rating, media), QnA (questions/answers), LoyaltyTransaction (points per unit)

### Geo‑nearby recommendations

- Store merchant and pickup lat/lon; PostGIS/earthdistance indexing.
- Ranking: distance, on‑hand inventory, base price per canonical unit, lead time/SLA, merchant rating, loyalty affinity.
- Fallbacks: regional hubs and pickup points when no local stock; auto split across nearby merchants if needed (partial shipments).

### Logistics approach

- Carrier abstraction: Addis couriers + national EMS/post + regional partners.
- Address capture: landmark + Plus Code + optional map pin; address book and delivery preferences.
- Delivery proof: OTP, driver photo, recipient signature; partial shipment handling; scheduled delivery windows where supported.

### Payments & escrow

- Telebirr: escrow intent → capture on delivery acceptance; dispute timer.
- Chapa: auth + webhook reconciliation; bank/card/mobile options where supported; diaspora card pay‑in → local delivery.
- Amole/CBE Birr: wallet push/pull; COD with reconciliation and limits.
- Edge cases: partial delivery/refund, failed delivery auto‑release rules; reservation “order now, pay at pickup/COD” with timed hold.

### Security & compliance

- KYC levels for merchant payouts; audit logs; least‑privilege RBAC.
- Idempotent payment webhooks; replay protection; checksum verification.
- VAT e‑invoices; consumer protection (returns/disputes SLAs).

### MVP (8–10 weeks)

- Multi‑merchant marketplace, unit‑aware catalog, inventory, cart/checkout.
- Payments: Telebirr + Chapa + COD; escrow basic; sandboxed Amole/CBE Birr if ready.
- Logistics: Addis + key regional corridors; shipment tracking, OTP, SMS updates.
- Merchant onboarding + dashboard v1; Amharic/English; PWA offline shell.
- Quick‑win differentiators (see below) prioritized for MVP where feasible.

### Phase 2 (6–8 weeks)

- Geo‑nearby recommendations v2 (auto‑split orders, SLA‑aware ETA), pickup points; rural batching.
- Full Amole/CBE Birr; returns/RMA; payouts center; VAT invoice center.
- Reviews with media; product Q&A; loyalty points/badges (unit‑based achievements).
- Back‑in‑stock waitlists; price‑drop alerts; wishlist & gift registry; reorder reminders.
- Diaspora gifting checkout (pay abroad, deliver locally); WhatsApp/SMS order bot.

### Phase 3 (ongoing)

- Group‑buy, haggle‑within‑bounds, bulk tier pricing for SMEs.
- Live shopping/creator tools; visual search by photo (CLIP‑style) for discovery.
- Subscriptions‑lite (recurring reminders + one‑tap reorder), then auto‑debit when rails support it.
- POS/ERP connectors; BOPIS with partner stores; pickup network expansion.

### Additional differentiators uncommon locally (prioritized by effort)

- Quick wins (2–6 days each):
- Back‑in‑stock waitlists and notifications (SMS/push).
- Price‑drop alerts and product watchlists.
- Wishlist and sharable gift registries.
- Product bundles and “frequently bought together” upsells (rule‑based).
- Lot/expiry tracking with near‑expiry deal tags for FMCG; “outlet” category.
- Address book, delivery instructions, scheduled delivery windows (where carrier supports).
- Reservation: order now, pay at pickup/COD with timed hold.
- Reorder from past orders + smart SMS reminders for staples.
- Unit‑aware filters and comparisons (e.g., ETB per kg/m/L across products).
- Product Q&A; seller answers flagged as official; moderation tools.
- Gift wrap and gift message at checkout.
- Diaspora gifting checkout (foreign card via Chapa) with local delivery.
- WhatsApp bot for order status/FAQ; SMS fallback for low‑data users.
- Medium effort (1–3 weeks):
- Auto split fulfillment across nearby merchants to complete order; partial shipments.
- Visual search by photo (open‑source model) for grocery/fashion discovery.
- BOPIS with partner merchants and pickup points network.
- Green/batched delivery slot discount (opt‑in, SLA‑aware).
- Size/fit recommendation (rules‑based first; ML later) for apparel.

### Risks & mitigations

- Addressing accuracy → Plus Codes + landmarks + OTP/photo proof.
- Payment reliability → multiple rails + robust webhooks + COD fallback.
- Delivery SLAs → proximity ranking + carrier SLAs + pickup points.

### KPIs

- GMV, orders; unit metrics (kg/L/m/piece sold); repeat rate; delivery SLA hit rate; cancellations/returns; merchant on‑time rate; NPS.
